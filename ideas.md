# SaveIQ design direction

## Theme name

**Quiet Momentum** — a calm, premium fintech interface that turns small, consistent actions into visible progress.

## Design movement

Editorial fintech with a soft neo-brutalist edge: confident blocks of navy, restrained emerald accents, and generous breathing room. The product should feel trustworthy before it feels clever.

## Core principles

1. Make progress legible at a glance.
2. Use contrast to guide action, not to create noise.
3. Celebrate consistency with restraint.
4. Keep every financial action confirmable and reversible where possible.
5. Treat accessibility as part of visual polish.

## Color philosophy

Deep navy anchors the system, warm paper neutrals keep surfaces approachable, emerald signals positive momentum, and a pale mint highlight marks opportunities. Red is reserved for destructive or risk states. Avoid gradients in the primary product UI.

## Layout paradigm

A compact editorial dashboard: generous outer margins, rounded 22px cards, layered surface hierarchy, and a persistent desktop rail that becomes a simple top bar on mobile. Charts should be short, wide, and readable.

## Signature elements

- A rising three-bar goal mark with a mint dot for the SaveIQ logo.
- Tiny milestone dots inside progress tracks.
- Short uppercase eyebrow labels above high-value metrics.
- “Quiet wins” activity language instead of noisy gamification.

## Interaction philosophy

Financial mutations wait for server confirmation before announcing success. Ordinary preferences can feel immediate. Form errors stay local, specific, and announced to assistive technology. Focus rings are always visible.

## Animation

Use 160–220ms transitions, progress fill easing, small milestone sparkles, and a gentle lift on hover. Avoid looping animation and avoid animation that changes meaning.

## Typography system

Use Inter or a system equivalent. Large display numerals are tight and semibold. Body copy is 15–16px with relaxed line height. Labels are compact, medium-weight, and slightly tracked.

## Brand essence and voice

SaveIQ is a calm coach for people building a future. Voice is direct, optimistic, and grounded: “You’re 3 weeks ahead” instead of “Amazing!!!”.

## Wordmark and logo

The wordmark is lowercase `saveiq` in a rounded semibold treatment. The icon is a full-bleed navy square with three ascending emerald bars and a pale mint milestone dot. The mark contains no baked-in outer mask, shadows, or gradients.

## Signature brand color

`#18B889` — Momentum Emerald.
